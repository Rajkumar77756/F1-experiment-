ENGINEERING REPORT

COMPUTATIONAL PERFORMANCE

A comprehensive breakdown of next-generation physical limits, structural simulations, and active computational fluid dynamics (CFD) implemented at the apex of motorsport.

01 / DYNAMICS

Advanced CFD & Aerodynamics

Modern Formula 1 cars are designed as inverted wings, utilizing underfloor ground effect Venturi tunnels to generate massive downforce. By creating a extreme low-pressure zone beneath the carbon fiber floor, the car is suctioned directly onto the asphalt, resulting in cornering forces exceeding 5G.

Active Drag Reduction Systems (DRS) pivot the upper rear wing flap by 85mm on high-speed straights. This sheds critical aerodynamic drag, boosting top speeds by up to 12 km/h and transforming the vehicle from a high-downforce cornering machine into a low-drag supersonic bullet.

02 / STRUCTURE

3D Chassis Engineering

The driver survival cell (monocoque) is cured in high-pressure autoclaves using military-grade pre-impregnated carbon fiber weaves interwoven with Kevlar layers.

This ultra-lightweight composite structure weighs under 100kg but survives crushing impact loads of over 250kN, ensuring structural integrity in extreme environments.

03 / POWERTRAIN

Precision Hybrid Units

The power unit is a marvel of thermal efficiency, surpassing 50% efficiency (compared to ~30% in standard passenger cars).

Combining a highly optimized 1.6-liter turbocharged V6 internal combustion engine with dual electric motor generators—the MGU-K (kinetic) and MGU-H (heat)—it instantly deploys over 1,000 combined horsepower.

04 / SENSORS

Real-Time Data Synthesis & Telemetry

Over 300 physical sensors integrated across the chassis and engine block stream multi-gigabit telemetry in real-time to the pit wall. Underfloor lasers map ride heights at 1,000 samples per second, compensating for tire compression, high-speed asphalt undulations, and aerodynamic downforce variations.

By merging real-time thermal tire imaging with predictive machine learning models, race strategists run thousands of micro-simulation scenarios per second, calculating optimal pit stop windows and predicting aerodynamic decay rates dynamically.

05 / STRUCTURE ASSEMBLY

Precision Engineering

Witness the culmination of aerospace materials curing. The monocoque chassis assembly is optimized down to the micrometer, ensuring that the 3D-molded carbon weaves withstand extreme torsional stress while keeping weight distribution optimally balanced near the center of gravity.

Every suspension component and internal dampening cylinder is integrated directly into the structural cell, maximizing space efficiency and enabling superior mechanical telemetry feedback on high-speed track sectors.

06 / TELEMETRY TEST

Unleashed on the Tarmac

Unlocking sheer velocity. The vehicle's aerodynamic body panels work in unison to manipulate airflow at high speed, turning surrounding currents into raw downforce. Real-time DRS adjustments ensure maximum velocity on long straights while maintaining massive stability in aggressive corners.

Tire temperature indicators and brake thermal maps track the immediate friction levels, feeding the engine control unit to deliver optimal power distribution under aggressive acceleration.
